# Measurement & Sizing Guide (`MEASUREMENT_GUIDE.md`)

## 1. Custom Bespoke Body Measurement Fields (Inches)
All bespoke sewing orders collect measurements in **inches (`in`)** with validation bounds to catch typos before cutting fabric.

| Field Key | Display Label | Measurement Instruction for Client | Min (in) | Max (in) | Required For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `bust` | Bust Circumference | Measure around the fullest part of the bust with tape parallel to the floor. | 26.0 | 60.0 | All tops & gowns |
| `underbust` | Underbust Circumference | Measure snugly directly beneath the bust line. | 22.0 | 54.0 | Corsets, bustiers, fitted gowns |
| `waist` | Natural Waist | Measure around the narrowest part of the torso above the navel. | 20.0 | 56.0 | All fitted outfits & skirts |
| `hip` | Hip Circumference | Measure around the fullest part of the hips and buttocks (approx. 8–9" below waist). | 28.0 | 68.0 | Gowns, skirts, trousers, wrappers |
| `shoulder_width` | Shoulder Width | Measure from the outer edge of the left shoulder bone to the right shoulder bone across the back. | 12.0 | 22.0 | All tops, kaftans & gowns |
| `half_length` | Half-Length (Shoulder to Waist) | Measure from the highest point of the shoulder over the bust point down to the natural waist. | 13.0 | 21.0 | Blouses, peplums, half-cut gowns |
| `blouse_length` | Blouse / Top Length | Measure from shoulder top down to desired blouse hem. | 16.0 | 32.0 | Blouses & two-piece tops |
| `sleeve_length` | Sleeve Length | Measure from shoulder tip to desired length (Short: 7–10", Elbow: 12–14", 3/4: 17–19", Long: 22–26"). | 4.0 | 28.0 | All sleeved garments |
| `armhole` | Armhole / Bicep | Measure around the fullest part of the upper arm. | 9.0 | 24.0 | Fitted sleeves |
| `skirt_length` | Skirt / Wrapper Length | Measure from natural waist down to knee, midi, or floor length. | 18.0 | 48.0 | Skirts & wrappers |
| `gown_length` | Full Gown Length | Measure from shoulder top over the bust straight down to desired hem (include heel height if applicable). | 34.0 | 68.0 | Full gowns & kaftans |

### Fit Preference Options
- `fit_preference`: `snug_fitted` | `standard_tailored` | `loose_flowing`
- `padding_preference`: `none` | `light_cup` | `structured_pushup`
- `lining_preference`: `full_cotton_lining` | `satin_lining` | `unlined`
